Our process

How we clean a roof

Four steps, and none of them involve a pressure washer on your shingles.

  1. 1

    Inspect

    We check every slope for damaged shingles, tiles and weak spots, so cleaning never makes a problem worse.

  2. 2

    Clean gently

    Non-destructive methods matched to your roof type. Nothing that dislodges shingles or pushes water underneath.

  3. 3

    Kill the growth

    Moss and algae root into roofing. We treat the growth itself so it doesn’t come right back next season.

  4. 4

    Keep it clean

    Yearly treatments protect the roof and help with insurance or HOA maintenance requirements.

FAQ

Questions before we climb up

Why won't you pressure-wash my roof?

High-pressure water can knock shingles loose, strip granules, force water under the roofing into the deck and attic, and void your manufacturer warranty. It also tends to blast off the surface growth and leave the roots behind. We use gentler, non-destructive methods chosen for your roof type.

Do I need a cleaning, a repair or a new roof?

Start with a free evaluation. If your roof can be cleaned or repaired, we'll tell you. If it's time to replace it, we'll show you why with photos and walk you through your options.

What roofing materials do you install?

Malarkey, Owens Corning and GAF. New roofs are backed by the manufacturer's warranty plus our own in-house workmanship guarantee.

How often should solar panels be cleaned?

Once a year for most homes. A thin layer of pollen and grime is enough to cut output, and some panel manufacturers require regular maintenance to keep the warranty valid.

Do you paint interiors too?

Yes. Anything from a single room to the whole exterior, with full surface prep and Sherwin-Williams or Benjamin Moore paint.

Do you take the holiday lights down?

Yes. We design, install, fix anything that goes out during the season, and take everything down afterward.
